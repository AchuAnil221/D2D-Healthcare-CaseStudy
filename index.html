The Insight That Shaped It

Healthcare systems often operate in isolation — pharmacies, logistics, and communication layers rarely connect efficiently.

This fragmentation leads to delays, inefficiencies, and poor visibility across the delivery chain.

D2D was envisioned as a unified platform to bring structure, speed, and reliability to healthcare logistics.

The Challenge

Healthcare delivery was fragmented.

Pharmacies had no visibility into driver location.

Patients had no idea when to expect their medication.

Administrators relied on spreadsheets and phone calls to keep everything moving.

The technology existed. The connection between everyone didn't.
